Hi, I'm Amir

I'm Amir Suckra, an aerospace engineering student at Bergen County Technical High School – Teterboro, where I study Aerospace, Aeronautical and Astronautical Engineering. Alongside high school, I've taken college-level engineering coursework through dual enrollment at William Paterson University, The University of Texas at Austin, and the New Jersey Institute of Technology.

Today I'm a Research Intern at Teachers College, Columbia University, helping research and develop science and math curriculum with Dr. Christopher Emdin, Professor of Science Education and Director of STEAM, DREAM & IdeaLab. Outside the classroom, I work as a river guide with Brooklyn to Alaska in Anchorage, Alaska.

Whether it's a 3D-printed puzzle cube, a SEAPERCH underwater ROV, or an airfoil design, I like taking ideas from concept to a working build — and I enjoy doing it with a team, through collaborative problem solving.
