About Me

Engineering student exploring aerospace, electronics, CAD modeling, and hands-on design.

Hi, I'm Amir

I'm Amir Suckra, an engineering student at Bergen County Academies who likes turning ideas into things that actually work. I'm most at home where aerospace, electronics, and design overlap — shaping an airfoil, simulating a circuit before it's built, or modeling a part in CAD and then holding the printed result in my hand.

My projects range from a SEAPERCH underwater ROV and drone-based aerial imaging to digital logic design, a 3D-printed mechanical puzzle cube, and a lensless pinhole camera. Each one taught me to plan carefully, test early, and iterate until the design holds up.
