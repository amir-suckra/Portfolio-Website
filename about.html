Hi, I'm Amir

Aerospace Engineering major at Bergen Tech · Class of 2027 · 3.978 GPA · National Honor Society

I'm Amir Suckra, an aerospace engineering student at Bergen County Technical High School – Teterboro, where I study Aerospace, Aeronautical and Astronautical Engineering. Alongside high school, I've taken college-level engineering coursework through dual enrollment at William Paterson University, The University of Texas at Austin, and the New Jersey Institute of Technology.

Today I'm a Research Intern at Teachers College, Columbia University, helping research and develop science and math curriculum with Dr. Christopher Emdin, Professor of Science Education and Director of STEAM, DREAM & IdeaLab. Outside the classroom, I'm a youth counselor and guide with Brooklyn to Alaska, leading groups of middle and high school students from New York City to Alaska's national parks. I also volunteer with the Joyful Youths Foundation, supporting elementary schools in Jamaica.

Whether it's a hardwood puzzle cube, a SEAPERCH underwater ROV, or an airfoil design, I like taking ideas from concept to a working build, and I enjoy doing it with a team. Next, I plan to study mechanical engineering in college, building on my CAD, MATLAB, and simulation skills to design and build real hardware.
