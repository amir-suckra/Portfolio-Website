Introduction to Aerospace Engineering

Puzzle Cube

CAD Woodworking Puzzle

Project Goal

Responding to a design brief from a fictional furniture maker, I turned 27 scrap ¾″ hardwood cubes into a desktop puzzle: five unique, interlocking pieces of four to six cubes each that assemble into a 2¼″ cube and challenge high school students.

What I Built

A working hardwood puzzle cube, plus a project presentation and build guide documenting the design.

Photos & Visuals

Build Process

  1. Read the brief. Listed the client's criteria: 27 ¾″ cubes, exactly five unique pieces of four to six cubes, some interlocking, forming a 2¼″ cube.
  2. Explore every option. Sketched the possible shapes that can be made from three, four, five, and six cubes.
  3. Choose the set. Picked five different pieces that fill a 3 × 3 × 3 cube and interlock.
  4. Build and check. Glued the hardwood cubes into pieces and confirmed they assemble into a solid cube.
  5. Document. Presented the design and wrote a build guide so someone else could reproduce it.

My Role

This was an individual design project. I worked from the client brief through sketching, piece selection, building, and documentation.

Key Problem Solved

Finding five different piece shapes that meet every rule in the brief, interlock, and still assemble into a solid cube without being too easy or too hard to solve.

What I Learned

Working from a client design brief, spatial reasoning, and documenting a build clearly enough for someone else to follow.
