Introduction to Aerospace Engineering

Airfoil Design

Aerospace CAD Airfoil

Project Goal

Designed and analyzed a wing section based on the NACA 23018 airfoil, the profile used on the Grumman F8F Bearcat. The project explored how airfoil geometry and angle of attack drive lift and drag.

What I Built

I used JavaFoil to calculate lift, drag, and moment coefficients from 0° to 30° angle of attack, then built a spreadsheet that predicts lift and drag forces at airspeeds from 10 to 60 mph. I scaled the airfoil coordinates to a 3.5-inch chord, imported them into Autodesk Inventor, and modeled the wing section for 3D printing.

Key Problem Solved

Balancing lift-to-drag ratio across different angles of attack while maintaining structural feasibility of the airfoil profile.

Skills Gained

Fundamentals of aerodynamics, how camber and thickness affect pressure distribution, and the iterative nature of engineering design.
